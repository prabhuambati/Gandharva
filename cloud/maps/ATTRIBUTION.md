Changes: extracted static path geometry and numeric transforms; removed original SVG scripts/styles/definitions, election/claims shading and labels; grouped Dadra and Nagar Haveli/Daman/Diu components for current selector use. No original executable SVG is bundled. The registry has 36 state/UT entries and 34 geometry groups; Andaman/Nicobar and Lakshadweep have selector entries but no extracted shape.

Retain attribution and the license link in future interactive presentations. The basemap is dated/illustrative, not an official boundary authority. It neither proves a creator's residence nor certifies current legal borders. Region membership must come from separately reviewed creator self-disclosure, never from map geometry or inference.
